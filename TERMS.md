# Terms of Use

PartyGames Bot is intended for safe, age-appropriate party games in Discord servers.

Users must not use the bot for harassment, NSFW content, hate speech, gambling, threats, or other unsafe behavior.

Server staff are responsible for configuring permissions, reviewing reports, and moderating their communities.

The bot includes content filtering and report tools, but no automated system is perfect. Use `/report` and moderator commands when needed.
